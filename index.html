The idea

Chronic illness shrinks the map.

The same few rooms. The pharmacy. The waiting room. The same conversation about the same subject.

This week, your body doesn't have to do a thing. Your attention does the traveling, and it goes somewhere new every day.

Each day has three parts: a place to go, something to do there, and a question. The question is the point. The place is how you get to it.

Your guide

Hi, I'm Mo.

My name is Mogene Christensen. I grew up in a small town with three older siblings and a younger sister, and even at four years old I remember making up stories. I've leaned toward creative things ever since.

I studied Exercise and Wellness with a minor in Psychology. In 2015 I was diagnosed with Multiple Sclerosis. I got married in 2018, graduated from college in 2019, and had a baby in 2021.

I made the Armchair Passport because life with a chronic illness deserves more than appointments and pill schedules. It deserves somewhere new to go.

Questions

Before you pack

Does it cost anything?

No. The 7-day adventure is free, and every tool it uses is free too.

What if I miss a day, or can't do it at all?

You can't fall behind. The emails will wait in your inbox. Do them in any order, at any pace, at any level. A few minutes of the Light version counts just as much.

What do I need?

A phone or a computer and an internet connection. You can do every day from bed.

Will I have to talk about my health?

Never. There are no check-ins and no symptom questions in the emails. Health is off the agenda all week.

When does it start?

As soon as you sign up. Day 1 arrives right away, then one new adventure each morning for the next six days.
